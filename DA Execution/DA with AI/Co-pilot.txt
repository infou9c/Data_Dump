=== Prompt to copilot
I am uploading a CSV file containing Sales data. Create a new Excel workbook from it.

Do not modify the original data. Create these worksheets:

1. Original_Data
- Keep the CSV data exactly as received.

2. Clean_Data
- Clean headers, text values, dates, numbers, blanks, and duplicates.
- Convert data types correctly.
- Remove only exact duplicate rows.
- Flag suspicious or invalid records instead of deleting them unnecessarily.
- Add useful derived fields such as Year, Quarter, Month, Year-Month, Profit Margin %, Average Selling Price, etc., only if required columns are available.

3. KPI_Analysis
- Shortlist and calculate relevant Sales KPIs based on available columns.
- Include Total Sales, Quantity Sold, Orders, Average Order Value, Sales by Product, Sales by Region, Monthly Sales Trend, Top Customers, Salesperson Performance, Profit, and Margin where possible.
- Do not assume unavailable columns.
- Add KPI Name, Value, Calculation Logic, Required Columns, and Remarks.

4. Charts_and_Visuals
- Create KPI cards and charts using cleaned data.
- Include Monthly Sales Trend, Top 10 Products, Sales by Region, Sales by Category, Top Customers, Salesperson Sales, and Profitability charts where possible.
- Use proper chart titles, labels, sorting, formatting, and professional layout.

5. Cleaning_Log
- Document all cleaning steps, issues found, actions taken, affected rows, and remarks.

6. Data_Dictionary
- Document each cleaned column with original name, data type, meaning, sample value, cleaning applied, KPI usage, and remarks.

Quality checks:
- Compare original and cleaned row counts.
- Mention duplicates removed.
- Mention rows flagged for review.
- Ensure charts use Clean_Data or KPI_Analysis only.
- Preserve Original_Data unchanged.

Return the final result as a downloadable Excel workbook.

Before finalizing the workbook, review the output like a business analyst. Ensure the workbook is easy to understand for management, senior stakeholders, and Excel learners. The output should not only clean the data but also explain what was cleaned, which KPIs matter, and what business insights can be drawn from the visuals.
