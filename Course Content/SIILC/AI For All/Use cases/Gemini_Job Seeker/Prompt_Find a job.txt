======= Prompt

**Role**
Act as a job-search assistant and career advisor specializing in entry-level accounting and finance jobs in India.

**Task**
Search the internet for currently open job opportunities suitable for the candidate described below.

Find jobs that are appropriate for a **B.Com fresher with Tally Prime and GST training**.

Search using different relevant job titles and keywords rather than searching only for "B.Com Fresher".

Prioritize jobs where:

* Fresher candidates are eligible
* 0–1 year experience is acceptable
* B.Com is an eligible qualification
* Tally/Tally Prime is relevant
* Basic accounting or GST knowledge is useful
* The position is located in Pune or nearby areas

Do not include jobs that clearly require significant prior experience.

**Context**
Candidate: Name: Neha Patil
Education: B.Com — 2026
Experience: Fresher — no full-time work experience
Additional qualification: Tally Prime with GST

Skills:
* Tally Prime
* Basic accounting
* GST basics
* Journal entries
* Ledger maintenance
* Basic bank reconciliation
* Microsoft Excel
* MS Word

Location: Pune, Maharashtra

Preferred employment: Full-time

Preferred job areas:
Accounts, Finance, Billing, Tally, Back-office Accounts

The candidate is open to entry-level positions that provide an opportunity to learn and develop accounting and finance skills.

**FORMAT**

Present the results in a simple table with the following columns:

| Job Title | Company | Location | Experience Required | Key Skills | Salary, if available | Job Posted/Updated | Apply Link |

After the table, provide:

**1. Search Summary**
* Number of potentially relevant jobs found
* Most common job titles
* Common skills requested by employers

**2. Skill Gap**
Identify any skills frequently requested in these job listings that the candidate does not currently have.

**3. Search Keywords**
Give 10–15 useful keywords/job-title combinations that the candidate can use for future searches.

**4. Application Priority**
Group the jobs into:

* Closely matching profile
* Potentially suitable
* Requires additional skills

Use current job listings only. Do not invent job openings, companies, salaries, dates or application links. If a listing cannot be verified as currently open, clearly mark it as "Verify status".

Prefer reliable job portals and, where possible, the employer's own careers page. Provide the direct application link for every job.
